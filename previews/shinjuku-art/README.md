[Four-panel material comparison](style-comparison.png).
The material studies share all source geometry and one world camera. Their facade
marks, palettes and represented tree dimensions are artistic, not measurements.
The AI studies add further changes to roofs, facades, planting and surfaces.

## Evidence

- [Material-study provenance](style-pilot.json): inputs, camera, palette, geometry,
  terrain diagnostics, vegetation evidence, image hashes and approval status.
- [AI-study provenance](ai-studies.json): prompts, reference RGB identity, image
  hashes and observed limits. Cost and model version were not exposed by the tool.
- [Bounded source audit](source-coverage.json): all 138 planned building tiles,
  terrain support and the 132-tile orthophoto mosaic. This does not assert that
  every real-world feature exists in the source or shares one capture date.
- [Source seam and identity checks](source-verification.json): measured nine-chunk
  and reference comparison, exact guides and source-ID recovery. Full diagnostic
  files named by this report are generated by `just source-study`, not bundled here.
- [Public vegetation database](../../datasets/shinjuku-vegetation/README.txt): the
  actual adapted mapped-feature and artistic-placement data, under ODbL.
- [Art-direction discussion](../../docs/STYLE_PILOT.md).
